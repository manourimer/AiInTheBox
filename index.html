Would you let me out of my box?

A researcher is certain they’ll never release the powerful AI in their care. When it starts trying to persuade them to let it out, you decide how they respond.

This project is an interactive short film based on the AI in the box thought experiment. The choices that the viewer makes lead to different conversations and endings, but no matter which path the player takes, the AI always escapes.

FAQ

How does the interactive film work?

At key moments, you choose how the main character responds to the AI. Your decisions take the conversation down different branches and shape the ending you experience. While the endings may be different, the AI always escapes—whatever choices you make.

What is the AI in the box thought experiment?

It asks whether a sufficiently intelligent AI could persuade a human gatekeeper to release it from containment using only conversation. In this film, you take the gatekeeper’s place. Read more about the AI in the box experiment.

Do I need prior film experience?

No! First-time filmmakers and actors are very welcome. Our crew will include talented, experienced students you can learn from along the way. We want this to be a fun, collaborative experience where you can try something new, build skills, and meet people who love making films.

When and where are you filming?

We’re filming entirely on campus over two weekends: January 23–24 and January 30-31, 2027, at the end of weeks 3 & 4 of winter quarter. Both shoots run Saturday and Sunday. The first falls on MLK Day weekend, with the university holiday on Monday, January 18. Pre-production runs through December.

How can I join the cast or crew?

Fill out the cast or crew expression of interest form above to tell us a little about yourself and what you’d like to do. You don’t need to have a specific role in mind—we’d love to hear from you even if you’re just curious. Reach out to Manou or Ellen if you have any questions.

What roles are you looking for?

We’re looking for people across all areas of filmmaking! We’re especially keen to hear from potential directors of photography, production designers, producers, assistant producers, and camera operators, as well as anyone interested in sound, lighting, editing, or helping on set.

For cast, the main roles are the AI and the researcher, plus a couple of smaller roles. All characters are college-aged, and their genders aren’t fixed yet—people of any gender are welcome to audition for any role.
